Mexican Oil Reserves

Mexico recently completed a new assessment of its oil reserves and estimated proven and probable oil reserves at approximately 15.7 billion barrels. Mexico’s earlier assessment in 2007 estimated reserves at approximately 12.4 billion barrels in all country resources, but in 2011, the estimate was revised to 15.7 billion barrels to reflect additional exploration findings.

Sigsbee Field

The Sigsbee Field is one of the largest gas and crude oil fields in the Gulf of Mexico, with new advances in seismic mapping and drilling leading to the discovery of this field. This offshore oil field is located approximately 150 km southwest from the city of Matamoros on the east coast of Mexico and is often referred to as “the Grand Canyon under the sea”.

Four Integral Components

The Sigsbee Field is comprised of the following four integral components. These components are responsible for the production, processing and transportation of oil and gas from the field.

Ojeda Oil and Gas Processing Plant

Ojeda is an oil and gas processing plant located east of San Fernando, Mexico. It is one of the largest crude oil and gas processing facilities in Mexico.
